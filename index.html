The Black Death

The black death was the most dangerous disease in all of history. It peaked around 1347 and killed over 33% of the European population. There are 3 vedrsions of this plague

Bubonic

The Bubonic plague consists of several symptoms such as swelling and severe headaches. Then the person will see black "buboes" which will explode.

Septicemic

Infects the bloodstream, being able to cause internal bleeding and darken tissue, hence the name "Black Death"

Pneumonic

Infects the lungs, spreading through the air through what would be "miasma". The person will then cough bloody mucus.

The Medival Times

The medival times was a time in history that lasted around 1000 years, it is the time of the falling of the roman empire and the transition to the modern era.

Notable People

Giovanni Boccaccio

He was an Italian writer who survived the Black Death in 1348 and wrote The Decameron. He is famous for describing what life was like during the plague.


Plague Doctors

These were doctors that had the long beaks for their mask. They had these long beaks so they could hide herbs and flowers.
